# PrivatePay demo video storyboard

**Format:** 42-second product walkthrough, 16:9, captions on screen.

## Scene 1 — The problem (0–7s)

**Visual:** Title card: “PrivatePay — private invoices, simple payouts.” Purple and gold brand colors.

**Voiceover:** “Need to collect payment for freelance work? PrivatePay lets you create a shareable invoice with a shielded Zcash destination.”

## Scene 2 — Sign in (7–14s)

**Visual:** PrivatePay login screen; select the Pollar email login, then show the signed-in Pollar wallet address. Use a fictional address in the video.

**Voiceover:** “Sign in with your Pollar account. PrivatePay uses the wallet attached to that account to identify your invoices.”

## Scene 3 — Create an invoice (14–21s)

**Visual:** Enter a Zcash testnet receiving address, ₦1,650 amount, ₦1,600 per ZEC rate, 0.00015% fee, and ‘Logo design’. Show the calculated freelancer payout and create the link.

**Voiceover:** “Add your shielded Zcash receiving address, invoice amount, exchange rate, fee, and a short description. PrivatePay calculates the payout and creates a shareable invoice link.”

## Scene 4 — Share and pay (21–28s)

**Visual:** Open the public invoice page, show the NGN total, calculated ZEC amount, and Copy Zcash address button. Then show a wallet’s testnet send screen with the raw address and amount entered separately.

**Voiceover:** “Share the invoice link with your client. They can see the amount due, copy the recipient address, and send the displayed ZEC amount from a compatible Zcash wallet on the matching network.”

## Scene 5 — Track and payout (28–35s)

**Visual:** Simple status animation: pending → payment submitted → payment confirmed → payout processing/completed. Mark payout as dependent on configured Zcash RPC.

**Voiceover:** “PrivatePay’s backend records invoice status and verifies payments. A real payout is attempted only when the server has a configured Zcash wallet connection, and is marked complete after verification.”

## Scene 6 — Close (35–42s)

**Visual:** Brand card with “Private invoices. Powerful payouts.” and “Zcash testnet demo”.

**Voiceover:** “PrivatePay: a simple way to invoice and receive shielded Zcash payments.”

## Production notes

- Treat all addresses and transaction details as fictional demo data. Never show a seed phrase, private key, email inbox, or real wallet balance.
- The current public invoice page copies the raw Zcash receiving address. The payer enters the displayed ZEC amount separately in their wallet; the page does not submit a transaction itself.
- The demo must identify this as a testnet flow. Real payouts require a configured Zcash RPC wallet on the backend.
