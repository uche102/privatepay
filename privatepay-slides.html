PrivatePay
Our solution

PrivatePay is a wallet-based invoicing app

Freelancers can:

  • connect their wallet
  • add a real shielded Zcash receiving address
  • create an invoice with amount and description
  • share a payment link with a client

Clients can:

  • open the invoice
  • pay via a Zcash payment request
  • track payment status clearly
PrivatePay
How it works

Simple workflow from invoice to payout

Step 1

Connect wallet

The freelancer authenticates using their wallet and defines their identity in the app.

Step 2

Create invoice

The user adds a shielded receiving address, amount, and description.

Step 3

Share and pay

The client opens the invoice and pays using a Zcash payment request.
